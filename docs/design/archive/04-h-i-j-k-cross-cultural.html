Principles
  • Primary palette only: yellow, blue, red, black, cream. No in-betweens.
  • Geometric shapes (circles, triangles) appear as decoration, not decoration — as structure.
  • 3 px thick keylines, square corners, no shadows, no gradients.
  • Futura-like Jost is the only typeface. Discipline through restriction.
  • Chinese inline in italic Jost-style — unusual but it works.
  • Risk: reads as "design school homework" to some; needs careful execution.
